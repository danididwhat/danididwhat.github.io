Accessibility

Last updated 17 November 2026

The short version

  • This site is built to be usable by everyone, including people who use a screen reader, a keyboard instead of a mouse, or zoom.
  • The goal is the WCAG 2.1 AA guidelines. A few known gaps are listed below.
  • If anything doesn't work for you, email dani@danididwhat.com and it will be fixed, or you will get the content another way.

What has been done

  • Screen readers: every photo has a written description, every film button says which film it plays, every form field has a label, and the page is divided into sections a screen reader can jump between.
  • Keyboard only: everything works with Tab, Enter, Escape and the arrow keys. The film player and photo viewer open with Enter, close with Escape, and return you to where you were. The moving rows of films and photos pause while you are on one of them.
  • Low vision: text is sized and coloured for readability, zooming is never blocked, and the page rearranges itself without sideways scrolling when zoomed in.
  • Motion: if your device is set to reduce motion, the moving rows, sparkles and short video clips stop.
  • Video: the short clips that play inside the phone frames are silent decoration. The full films play in YouTube's player, which has its own captions and keyboard controls.

How it was checked

With an automated accessibility checker (axe), which found no problems on this site's pages, and by hand: keyboard-only use, zoom, and reduced motion, in Chrome. It has not yet been tested with every screen reader and browser combination.

Known gaps

  • A few decorative gold words in the large headings are lower in contrast than the rest of the text. The same words are also readable by screen readers.
  • The rows of films and photos move on their own. They pause when you hover over them, touch them, or move to them with the keyboard, and stop entirely with reduce-motion turned on, but there is no separate pause button yet.
  • Captions on the full films are being checked. If a film has no captions and you would like to know what is said in it, email and you will get a written version.
  • The contact form uses Cloudflare's human check. It usually needs no action from you; if it ever stops you, email dani@danididwhat.com instead.

Tell us about a problem

Email dani@danididwhat.com with the page and what happened. You will get a reply, and the problem will be fixed where possible.
