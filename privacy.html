Privacy

Last updated 16 November 2026

The short version

  • If you send an enquiry, your details are used only to reply to you.
  • Nothing is sold, shared for marketing, or added to a mailing list.
  • Enquiries are deleted automatically 24 months after they arrive, or sooner if you ask.
  • This site sets no cookies of its own and uses no advertising or tracking pixels.

Who runs this site

danididwhat.com is the portfolio of Dani Did What?, the UGC (user-generated content) business of Daniela. For anything about your information, email dani@danididwhat.com.

What the contact form collects

Only what you type into it: your name, email address, phone number (optional), brand or company, what you need, and your message, plus the date and time you sent it.

It is used for one thing: replying to your enquiry. Your phone number, if you give it, is used only to contact you about that enquiry, never for marketing calls or texts.

Where it goes, and for how long

  • The enquiry is stored in a database run by Cloudflare, which hosts the form. It is deleted automatically 24 months after it arrives.
  • A copy is emailed to Daniela's inbox (Google Gmail), so she can reply. It is kept there as ordinary business correspondence, and deleted if you ask.

Spam protection

When you use the form, Cloudflare Turnstile checks that you are a person and not a bot. To do that it receives your IP address and some information about your browser and device. To stop floods of messages, the form also counts how many enquiries come from each IP address. That count is deleted after about an hour, and your IP address is never stored with your enquiry.

Visiting the site

This site sets no cookies of its own and stores nothing in your browser. It has no advertising, no tracking pixels, and no chat or session-recording tools. Like any website, it loads some things from other companies, and each of them receives your IP address and basic browser information when it does:

  • GitHub Pages hosts the site. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security.
  • Cloudflare serves the photos and short video clips, runs the contact form, and provides visitor statistics. The statistics count visits without cookies and do not identify you.
  • Google serves the fonts, and YouTube (owned by Google) serves the film cover images.
  • YouTube's player loads only when you tap a film, in YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode. From that point YouTube's own privacy policy applies, and YouTube may store information on your device.
  • The Instagram links are ordinary links. Nothing from Instagram loads on this site.

Their policies: GitHub, Cloudflare, Google and YouTube.

Tracking across other sites, and "Do Not Track"

This site does not track you across other websites, and does not let advertising networks do so. Google, as a provider of the fonts, cover images and video player, can see that your browser loaded them, as it can on any site that uses them. Because this site does no tracking of its own, it behaves the same whether or not your browser sends a "Do Not Track" signal.

Seeing, correcting or deleting your information

Email dani@danididwhat.com from the address you used in your enquiry. Daniela will tell you what is held, correct it, or delete it from the database and her inbox, and confirm when it is done.

Children

This site is for brands and businesses. It is not aimed at children under 13, and does not knowingly collect their information.

Changes to this page

If this page changes, the date at the top changes with it. A change in what is collected or how it is used will be described here before it takes effect.
