About

Hi, I'm Dani

I make short-form video and photos that look like they belong in your customer's feed. Nothing staged or stiff: the kind of content people stop scrolling for because it looks like real life.

That's because it is my real life. Weekends are Disney and Universal, days out on the boat, and three Samoyeds who are convinced every shoot is about them. At home there's a kitchen I actually cook in and rooms that are ready for a camera. Your product gets filmed where it would really be used, by someone really using it.

By day I work in engineering sales, so I'm used to learning a product properly and explaining it in plain words. I read the brief, ask the right questions and deliver on time. You get the creative side and the professional side in one person.
